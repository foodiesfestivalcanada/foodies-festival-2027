EXHIBITOR OPPORTUNITIES

Choose your way
to exhibit.

Three exhibitor formats are available. Select your preferred type and discuss your specific requirements during the application and booking process.

01FLEXIBLE SETUP

Space-only pitches

£200 per square metre
+ VAT

Flexible space for exhibitors to create an approved stand setup.

  • Suitable for an approved bespoke gazebo, up-cycled horsebox or renovated vehicle
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing
  • Power supply and storage available at additional cost
02MARQUEE SPACE

Exhibitor marquees

£247 per square metre
+ VAT

An open marquee with rustic accent back panelling.

  • Built-in roofing and flooring as standard
  • Prime location within the centre of the festival site
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing
  • Power supply and storage available at additional cost
03SMALL PRODUCERS

Artisan producers market

£395 per stall
+ VAT

A Farmers’ Market-style option intended for small producers selling food produce.

  • Countertop table supplied
  • Located between the festival entrance and Chefs Theatre
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing
i

Prices are shown exactly as supplied and are subject to VAT as stated. Additional services such as electricity and storage may cost extra.
