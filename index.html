Frequently asked questions

Helpful information for businesses considering an application. If you need clarification about a specific event, please contact the organising team before submitting or paying.

1. Who can apply to exhibit at Foodies Festival 2027?

Food and drink producers, artisan businesses, independent brands and other relevant exhibitors may submit an application. Your products and proposed display should be suitable for a food and drink festival environment. Applications are reviewed by the organising team and submission does not automatically guarantee acceptance.

2. Which festival locations are currently listed?

The dates shown on this website are Brighton (1–3 May 2027), Bath (21–23 May 2027), London at Syon Park (29–31 May 2027), and St Albans (4–6 June 2027). Each event has its own application link in the Festival Dates section.

3. How do I apply for a space?

Choose your preferred location and click its application button. Complete the relevant online form with accurate business details, product information and any requested supporting material. Submit one application for the event or events you wish to be considered for, following the instructions in the form.

4. Does submitting an application mean my business is confirmed?

No. An application is an expression of interest and must be reviewed. Any offer of space remains subject to suitability, availability and approval by the organisers. Wait for confirmation and official booking instructions before treating your participation as confirmed.

5. What exhibitor space options and prices are listed?

The supplied guide prices are space-only pitches at £200 per square metre plus VAT, exhibitor marquees at £247 per square metre plus VAT, and artisan producers market stalls at £395 plus VAT. These prices should be reconfirmed with the organisers, including the precise space size, what is included and any additional charges.

6. What is the difference between a space-only pitch and an exhibitor marquee?

A space-only pitch provides the allocated area for an exhibitor to arrange an appropriate stand or display. The exhibitor marquee option is described as an open marquee with rustic accent back panelling and built-in roofing and flooring. Confirm the exact specification, dimensions, permitted setup and any equipment requirements for your event before booking.

7. Are the listed prices inclusive of VAT?

No. The prices displayed for space-only pitches, exhibitor marquees and artisan producers market stalls are shown before VAT. VAT is additional. Ask the organising team for the full amount payable and an invoice or payment breakdown before making a payment.

8. Can I choose my own pitch size or location?

You may be able to indicate your preferred requirements in the application, but final allocation is managed by the organisers and depends on the event layout, availability, safety considerations and exhibitor suitability. Do not assume a requested position or size is reserved until it is confirmed in writing.

9. What information should I prepare before applying?

Prepare your business name and contact details, a clear description of your products, the event location you are interested in, your preferred exhibitor format and any requested photographs or supporting information. If you have special display, power or operational requirements, describe them accurately in the application and ask the organisers to confirm what can be accommodated.

10. Can I apply for more than one festival location?

You can express interest in multiple locations using the relevant application forms. Make sure you complete the correct form for each event and provide consistent business information. Each application may be assessed separately, and availability and approval may differ by location.

11. When will I know whether my application has been successful?

Application review and response timing may vary by event and the number of applications received. Check the email address you supplied, including your spam or junk folder. If you have not received an update, contact the organisers and identify the event and business name used in your application.

12. When should I make a payment?

Only make a payment after you have received official confirmation and the organisers have provided the correct payment instructions and amount. Confirm the payment deadline, deposit or balance requirements, VAT treatment and any cancellation or refund terms in writing. This website does not collect payments.

13. Are electricity, water, tables, chairs or other equipment included?

These inclusions are not specified here and may vary by pitch type and event. Contact the organisers before applying or booking to confirm whether electricity, water, furniture, refrigeration, waste facilities or other services are available, whether they incur additional fees, and what equipment you must provide yourself.

14. Can I sell food or drink that needs preparation or sampling?

Describe your products and proposed activities fully in your application. Requirements for food preparation, sampling, storage, hygiene, safety and permissions may apply. Ask the organisers what documentation and operating arrangements are required for your specific products and event. Do not assume that an application alone grants permission to trade or sample.

15. What if I need to update my application?

If you need to correct business details, change your product range or update a requirement after submitting, contact the organising team using the email below. Include the event name, business name and the email address used on the application so the team can identify your submission.

16. What if an event or pitch type is no longer available?

Space is subject to availability and organiser approval. If your preferred event or option is unavailable, ask whether another location, pitch type or suitable alternative is available. Do not assume that availability at one festival means the same option is available at another.

17. Where can I get further information?

For application questions, space specifications, availability, payment terms or event-specific requirements, email the Foodies Festival enquiries team at enquiries.foodiesfestival.org@gmail.com. Include the festival location and a concise description of your enquiry.
