EXHIBITOR OPPORTUNITIES

Find your space.

Choose the exhibitor format that best suits your business. All allocations are subject to availability, suitability and organiser approval.

Space-only pitches

£200 per square metre + VAT

  • Flexible space for an approved stand setup
  • Suitable for a bespoke gazebo, up-cycled horsebox or renovated vehicle
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing
  • Power and storage available at additional cost

Exhibitor marquees

£247 per square metre + VAT

  • Open marquee with rustic accent back panelling
  • Roofing and flooring included as standard
  • Prime location within the festival site
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing
  • Power and storage available at additional cost

Artisan producers market

£395 per stall + VAT

  • For small producers selling food produce
  • Farmers’ Market-style stall with countertop table supplied
  • Located between the festival entrance and Chefs Theatre
  • 50-word listing in the Festival Showguide
  • 20 complimentary tickets for pre-event marketing

Prices are shown as supplied. Electricity and storage may incur additional charges. Confirm availability and final requirements with the organising team.
